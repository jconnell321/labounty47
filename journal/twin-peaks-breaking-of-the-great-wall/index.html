Twin Peaks, the End of the Cycle and the Breaking of the Great Wall

Essay I · 2026

An exploration of Twin Peaks: The Return through Traditionalist metaphysics, counter-initiation and the dissolution of the boundaries separating the human world from darker spiritual realities.

David Lynch and Mark Frost’s Twin Peaks presents a world in which the apparent stability of ordinary existence is repeatedly breached by forces that resist rational explanation.

This essay will examine those breaches through the Traditionalist idea of the end of a cycle, drawing particular attention to René Guénon’s account of fissures in the Great Wall and the emergence of counter-initiatory forces.
